# Instagram Follow Check

Chrome extension that compares your Instagram **followers** and **following** using your already logged-in browser session. No passwords.

## What it shows

- People who follow you back
- People you follow who don’t follow back (**Remove**)
- People who follow you that you don’t follow (**Follow back**)

## Install

1. Open `chrome://extensions`
2. Enable **Developer mode**
3. **Load unpacked** → select this folder
4. Open [instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com/) and log in
5. Click **Follow Check** (top right) → **Scan my lists**

## Notes

- Works only while you’re logged into Instagram in that tab
- Large lists take longer; requests are paced to reduce rate limits
- Reload the extension and refresh Instagram after updates
